# Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** August 21, 2026

aisitey ("we," "us," or "our") operates aisitey.com (the "Site"). This
Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and
the choices you have.

## Information We Collect

The only personal information we currently collect is the **email
address** you provide if you subscribe to our newsletter.

We do not require an account to browse the Site, and we do not collect
names, payment information, or any other personal details through the
Site itself.

## How We Use Your Information

Your email address is used solely to:

- Send you updates about new templates, features, and tips related to
  aisitey.
- Respond to you if you contact us directly.

We do not sell, rent, or trade your email address to third parties.

## Data Storage

Email addresses submitted through the newsletter form are stored securely
using Supabase, a third-party database provider. Access to this data is
restricted — the newsletter signup form can only add new entries; it
cannot read or display existing subscriber data.

## Third-Party Services

The Site links to and integrates with the following third-party services,
each with its own privacy practices:

- **GitHub** — where our open-source code is hosted.
- **npm** — where our package is published.
- **Supabase** — where newsletter subscriber emails are stored.

We encourage you to review the privacy policies of these services if you
interact with them directly.

## Cookies and Tracking

The Site does not currently use advertising cookies or third-party
tracking scripts. If this changes in the future, this policy will be
updated accordingly.

## Your Choices

- **Unsubscribe:** Every newsletter email includes an unsubscribe link.
  You can also contact us directly to request removal.
- **Data deletion:** You may request that we delete your email address
  from our records at any time by contacting us.

## Children's Privacy

The Site is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly
collect personal information from children.

## Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be
posted on this page with an updated effective date.
